# 9inetales

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creators are the first priority. They supply the work that makes the platform
valuable to readers. The creator audience includes independent comic artists and
serial-fiction writers who want to submit work and reach readers.

Readers are also a major audience. Their discovery of stories and continued
engagement are central to the platform's success. Prioritising creators must
preserve a clear, useful reader path.

## Product Purpose

9inetales is an upcoming home for original comics and serial novels by African
creators. The current project is its waitlist website, separate from the reading
and publishing platform.

The waitlist is intended to build creator and reader interest ahead of onboarding.
Creator participation supplies the content needed to attract and retain readers.
Success means helping both audiences understand the offer and find their relevant
next step, with creators receiving first priority. Numerical targets are undecided.

## Positioning

The existing platform direction starts with Nigerian creators and readers, with a
wider African and global ambition.

The founder has not yet defined the specific differentiation from other comics
and fiction platforms. Do not turn this geographic focus or planned features into
unsupported claims of uniqueness or superiority.

## Operating Context

Visitors can learn about the reader experience, creator participation, the planned
publishing journey, frequently asked questions, and waitlist privacy.

The form supports reader, creator, and both roles. Audience actions preselect the
relevant role. Joining a future interest list is distinct from submitting work or
agreeing to publishing terms.

This repository contains the independent Next.js App Router waitlist application,
using TypeScript, React, and plain CSS. The design-kit site and brand masters live
separately in `/Users/user/Desktop/9ineTales design`. Each project keeps its own
required assets and tokens; neither should depend on the other at runtime.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The deployed signup is a demonstration. Brevo double opt-in collection and
  shared Redis request limits are implemented locally but remain disabled until
  provider credentials, sender/template, and operator privacy settings are ready.
  Live mode does not collect the optional format question.
- The reading platform and creator submission portal are not available in this app.
- Publishing review, chapter releases, and a curated pilot describe the planned
  journey, not implemented services.
- Offline reading is planned. Advertising-supported access and creator revenue
  sharing are under exploration; access rules and commercial terms are unconfirmed.
- No launch date, pilot eligibility, confirmed catalogue, earnings, payment rates,
  or payout schedule has been established.
- Do not invent testimonials, audience counts, partnerships, or creator work.
- The privacy notice must match actual information handling. Before enabling
  collection, implement the signup service and update the notice with real
  operator, provider, retention, contact, and unsubscribe information.
- Normal Next.js development and production builds are supported. Optional static
  output is generated by `npm run export`; a static site needs a separate backend
  for real signup collection.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the 9inetales name and the established identity unless a change is
explicitly requested. Use clear, inviting language for readers and creators.
Distinguish current functionality from planned functionality without unsupported
promises. Describe the privacy page as waitlist privacy rather than a design review.

## Product Principles

1. Prioritise creator participation because creator content sustains the platform.
2. Keep readers central: content should help people discover stories and return.
3. Make reader and creator paths clear while keeping interest registration
   separate from submissions and publishing commitments.
4. State capabilities and information handling truthfully at every stage.
5. Keep the waitlist and design kit independently maintainable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve labelled controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, responsive
layouts, text enlargement, and reduced-motion support. Moving content must have a
pause control and a static reduced-motion alternative. Comfortable mobile reading
and smaller downloads are part of the planned product direction; do not claim
offline support or low-bandwidth performance that has not been implemented.
