## FAQs

Is 9inetales available to read now? The reading platform is in development. We have not announced a launch date.

Will there be comics and novels? That’s the plan: original comics and serial novels, starting with a curated pilot. The first collection and supported formats will be confirmed as we test with creators and readers.

Will reading be free? We’re exploring a free, advertising-supported starting experience, including optional rewarded access. The access model is still being tested and will be explained before launch.

Can I read offline? Saved offline chapters are a planned feature. We’ll test download sizes, supported devices, and any access limits before confirming what will be available.

How will creators get paid? We’re developing a revenue-sharing model. Joining the waitlist does not qualify someone for payment. The calculation, eligibility, payout threshold, and schedule will be published before monetised participation.

Can I submit my work yet? This is a creator interest list, not a submission portal. Submission dates, requirements, and publishing agreements will be shared before the pilot opens.

Is this only for Nigerian creators? Our first focus is Nigerian creators and readers, with a wider African and global ambition. Specific pilot eligibility will be announced when submissions open.
