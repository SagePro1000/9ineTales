## Reusable design recipe

- Canvas: portrait 4:5; recommended export 1080 × 1350 px. Keep important elements at least 64 px from the edges at that size.
- Palette: paper #FFF8F0, orange #FF5A1F, ink #211A17. Neutral surfaces dominate.
- Display type: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold. Supporting type: Source Sans 3.
- Structure: small campaign strap; large ink-framed illustration; short bold headline; one supporting sentence; logo and orange call to action; development status.
- Signature detail: angular speech-bubble geometry from the logo, selective orange halftone, and a single offset ink shadow.
- Swap the illustration, headline, and supporting sentence for future posts; retain typography, margins, footer, colours, and logo clear space.
- Use the provided logo without distortion. The generated poster is a visual concept; original SVG files remain the source of truth for production logo artwork.
- Label generated concept art; replace with permissioned, credited creator artwork when available. Do not imply a live catalogue or open submissions.
