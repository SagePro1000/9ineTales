# 9inetales brand foundations — version 0.1

Status: proposed identity and review-ready copy, September 2026. This is not final founder approval or trademark clearance.

## Positioning

9inetales is an upcoming home for original comics and serial novels by African creators, starting in Nigeria.

Audience: readers interested in compelling original stories and independent comic artists and writers interested in publishing. Nigerian creators and readers are the first focus. Reader age policy remains to be resolved before a live waitlist or platform collects data.

Visual thesis: bold Nigerian storytelling, with editorial clarity and warmth. Confident, imaginative, welcoming, and respectful of creators. Expressive marketing, quiet reading surfaces, precise commercial information.

Primary campaign: “Our stories. New worlds.”
Descriptor: “Comics and novels. Nigerian voices. New worlds.”
Supporting line: “Original voices. A world of possibility.”

## Logo family

The original raster artwork is preserved as original-logo.png. The supplied logo inspired new flat vector redraw studies, not exact traces. Barlow Condensed ExtraBold letterforms are outlined in the exports, so the SVGs do not require installed fonts.

- primary.svg: two-colour stacked speech-bubble signature, retaining the spark motif.
- primary-ink.svg and primary-reversed.svg: single-colour alternatives.
- horizontal.svg, horizontal-ink.svg, horizontal-reversed.svg: header and narrow-space wordmarks.
- symbol.svg, symbol-ink.svg, symbol-reversed.svg: simplified numeral-nine bubble.

Minimum starting sizes: primary 160px wide; horizontal 140px wide; symbol 32px for regular use, 16px for favicon only. Test the actual placement. Clear space: at least one quarter of the symbol height around the logo.

Use flat approved colours. Preserve aspect ratio. Do not distort, add gradients, redraw the symbol separately, or place directly on busy artwork. For backgrounds without sufficient contrast, use a neutral containment surface or the reversed version.

Calculated contrast: ink/orange 5.50:1; white/dark orange 5.14:1; ink/paper 16.28:1; muted/paper 5.93:1. White/signature orange is 3.12:1, so it is unsuitable for normal-size text. Always assess adjacent colours and actual usage.

Keep neutral surfaces dominant. Creator artwork is allowed to have its own palette.

## Typography

Display: Barlow Condensed, weights 700 and 800. Short headlines and section titles only.
Body/UI: Source Sans 3, weights 400, 600, and 700. Paragraphs, labels, navigation, forms, and explanations.

Desktop headline range 56–116px; section titles 44–78px; subheads 20–24px; body 16–20px, line height 1.5–1.65; metadata generally 14px. Responsive sizes should use rem/clamp and remain usable with 200% text enlargement. A few compact prototype disclosures use 12–13px and must remain secondary.

Sentence case for most UI. Uppercase for brief labels and expressive campaign headlines. Do not use display type for novel chapters. The future reader font requires long-session and language coverage testing with actual material, including Nigerian diacritics.

Fonts are locally served, with TTF originals and compact WOFF2 files. Source and licence references are in FONT-LICENSES.md.

## Layout and components

Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px. Main max-width 1240px. Desktop gutters 56px; mobile 20px.

2px corner radius. Ink borders for essential controls; lower-contrast lines only for nonessential separators. Offset shadows selectively on calls to action and illustrative frames, not on every paragraph or card.

Primary button: signature orange / ink text / 2px ink border / 4px ink offset shadow. Secondary action: underlined text, clear focus style. Radio options: visible selected state and native keyboard handling. Email: persistent label, autocomplete, inline errors. Consent: explicit unchecked checkbox. FAQ: native details/summary.

Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep content visible without animation. Do not use decorative movement that consumes attention or delays reading.

## Imagery

Use licensed or commissioned creator covers, panels, and excerpts with credits and permission covering the placement. Do not distort lettering or crop important content. Avoid fake catalogues, fake testimonials, and invented launch statistics.

Use references grounded in the particular story, rather than generic continent-wide symbols. Do not suggest representation of a named deity without story context and research.

The current hero and imagery sample use supplied comic splash artwork, credited to @mohammedawwall from the supplied filename. It is a visual reference, not a confirmed 9inetales catalogue title or partnership. The full composition is preserved in the waitlist. Prefer commissioned or licensed participating-creator artwork with agreed credits for launch. The earlier AI concept and its generation prompt remain archived for provenance; they are no longer used in the active pages.

## Voice and claims

Reader: curiosity, characters, stories, discovery. Creator: requirements, rights, realistic benefits, and transparent terms.

State “upcoming” and “in development.” Say “planned” for offline features and “exploring” for advertising/revenue share. No guaranteed launch date, earnings, ownership arrangement, approval time, download period, or 60/40 split is presented as settled.

A creator interest signup is not a work submission and grants no rights. A production creator agreement must explicitly specify ownership, publishing licences, and removal rights before onboarding.

## Design workflow

Product brief → boundaries and claims → one visual direction → concrete prototype → specific critique → preserve approved choices → test mobile/interactions/accessibility → implement production collection when ready.

Use the supplied AI-Design-Workflow.pdf as an exploration reference; its sample prompts and model recommendations are not binding product requirements. Maintain written tokens and components so image exploration does not cause silent brand drift.
