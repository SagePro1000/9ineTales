# Review and launch checks

## Test the identity and page together

Recruit 5 target readers and 3–5 creators, separate from the founder. This is a small qualitative study, not market validation.

1. Show the opening for five seconds. Ask what 9inetales offers, who it is for, and whether it is available now.
2. Ask each person to find the information relevant to them and reach signup without prompting.
3. Ask a creator what joining grants the platform, whether payment is guaranteed, and how publishing would work. Correct misunderstandings through copy changes.
4. Ask a reader whether offline reading exists now. They should understand it is planned.
5. Ask people to recall and spell the name after closing the page. Record errors separately from pronunciation.
6. Compare the original logo with the proposed redraw in actual header, profile, and single-colour placements. Ask about readability and fit for both comics and novels.
7. Try the page on a low-end Android device and an iPhone, including slow connectivity and text enlargement.

Record task completion, hesitation, unexpected interpretations, and specific issues. Fix repeated comprehension problems before debating minor visual preferences.

## Prototype acceptance

- No horizontal clipping at 320, 360, 390, 768, and 1440px.
- Clear reader/creator routes and visible development status.
- Role selection, invalid email, missing consent, success, and reset states.
- Creator CTA preselects Creator.
- Native keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled inputs, and expandable FAQs.
- No prototype email storage, network submission, analytics, or ad scripts.
- Locally served fonts and imagery; broken asset and route checks.
- Reduced-motion support and readable text enlargement.
- Generated concept art explicitly labelled, not represented as a launch catalogue.

## Before collecting real emails

Choose a mailing-list provider and configure a production submit endpoint. Use server-side validation, spam protection, deduplication, explicit consent records, and appropriate email verification. Keep provider secrets server-side.

Replace the prototype notice with an operator-specific privacy notice and contact route. Resolve age policy, retention, provider/data-transfer arrangements, and unsubscribe handling. Replace the demo action and confirmation with truthful pending/confirmed, error, duplicate, and loading states. Test a full signup and unsubscribe with controlled test addresses before public release.

Decide whether optional interests are worth collecting; keep the initial form minimal. Segment reader/creator/both without making people join duplicate lists.

Select licensed launch imagery, verify founder approval of branding and page copy, and perform name/domain checks. No manufactured testimonials, metrics, titles, earnings, or launch dates.
